Open Global School was built for students in grades six through twelve who want a complete alternative to the traditional classroom, without losing the structure, feedback and teacher attention that real learning needs.

Five subjects, one timetable

Every grade from six to twelve follows the same core structure, taught by subject specialists.

Mathematics

From algebra and geometry to pre-calculus, taught step by step with practice sets after every live class.

Science

Physics, chemistry and biology, explained with experiments students can follow from home.

Technology

Computer fundamentals, programming and digital tools, building toward a real project each term.

Social Studies

History, geography and civics, connecting the lesson to events happening right now.

Languages & Arts

English and Bangla language skills alongside reading, writing and creative work.

Live Classes

Join real-time lessons with your teacher and classmates, and rewatch any class you missed.

Student Email & Storage

Every student gets an institutional email address and cloud storage for notes and assignments.

Dedicated Teachers

Small class groups taught by subject specialists who track every student's progress.
